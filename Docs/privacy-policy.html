Privacy Policy

This app was created for the Edinburgh IndiePop Collective and does not collect, use, store, or share personal information from users.

The app does not include analytics SDKs, crash reporting SDKs, advertising SDKs, or tracking SDKs.

The app retrieves publicly available event and news content and images from an online source for display within the app. No personal information is transmitted from the app to this source.

If you choose to open external links, such as links to social media, YouTube, or Google Maps, your browser or the relevant app handles that request under its own privacy policy.
